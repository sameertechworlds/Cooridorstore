Belated ITR for FY 2025-26: Last Date 31 December 2026, Late Fee and Steps

Missed the ITR deadline? File a belated return for AY 2026-27 by 31 December 2026. Late fee, interest, what you lose, filing steps, revised return and ITR-U.

Updated 28 November 2026 · 7 min read · By the Cooridor Store team

Key takeaways

  • Belated return for FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27) can be filed until 31 December 2026.
  • Late fee: ₹1,000 if total income is up to ₹5 lakh, otherwise ₹5,000, plus 1% a month interest on unpaid tax.
  • A belated return is filed under the new regime only, and most losses cannot be carried forward.
  • Missed 31 December too? Updated return (ITR-U) is possible until 31 March 2031 with extra tax.

Late fee and interest

ChargeAmount
Late fee, total income up to ₹5 lakh₹1,000
Late fee, total income above ₹5 lakh₹5,000
Interest on unpaid tax (Section 234A)1% per month or part, from the due date
Interest for short advance tax (234B, 234C)1% per month, if applicable

If your income is below the basic exemption limit and you are filing only to claim a refund, the late fee generally does not apply.

What you lose by filing late

  • Old regime option: if you do not have business income, you must choose the old regime on or before the original due date. A belated return is filed under the new regime, so deductions like HRA, 80C and home-loan interest are lost.
  • Carry-forward of losses: business losses and capital losses of the year cannot be carried forward. Loss from house property can still be carried forward.
  • Refund interest: interest on your refund is lower because it runs only from the date of filing.

How to file a belated return, step by step

  1. Download AIS, TIS and Form 26AS from the e-filing portal and check all income and TDS.
  2. Pay the late fee and any tax due through e-Pay Tax as self-assessment tax. Keep the challan details.
  3. Go to e-File → Income Tax Returns → File Income Tax Return, select AY 2026-27 and mode Online.
  4. Choose the filing section 139(4), belated return, and pick the correct ITR form.
  5. Check pre-filled data, add the challan under taxes paid, and confirm the late fee shows as paid.
  6. Submit and e-verify within 30 days using Aadhaar OTP or net banking. An unverified return is treated as not filed.

Made a mistake? Revised return

Budget 2026 extended the time to revise a return. For AY 2026-27 you can file a revised return until 31 March 2027, including revising a belated return. A fee may apply if you revise after 31 December 2026.

Missed 31 December as well? ITR-U

An updated return (ITR-U) can be filed up to four years from the end of the assessment year, so until 31 March 2031 for AY 2026-27. You pay extra tax of 25% to 70% of the tax and interest, depending on how late you file. ITR-U cannot be used to claim a refund or reduce tax. Filing before 31 December is almost always cheaper.

Why file at all? A return is needed for loans, visas and tenders, to claim refunds, and to avoid a notice when your AIS shows income such as interest, dividends or share sales.

Frequently asked questions

What is the last date to file a belated ITR for FY 2025-26?

31 December 2026, or before the assessment is completed, whichever is earlier.

What is the late fee for filing ITR after the due date?

₹1,000 if total income is up to ₹5 lakh and ₹5,000 above that, under Section 234F, plus interest on any unpaid tax.

Can I choose the old regime in a belated return?

If you have no business income, the old regime must be chosen by the original due date. A belated return is therefore filed under the new regime.

Can I get a refund in a belated return?

Yes. Refunds are allowed in a belated return, although interest on the refund is lower.

Can I revise a belated return?

Yes. For AY 2026-27 a belated return can be revised until 31 March 2027.

What if I miss 31 December 2026?

You can file an updated return (ITR-U) until 31 March 2031 by paying extra tax, but it cannot be used for a refund.
