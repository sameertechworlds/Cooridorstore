Why you need rent receipts

Salaried employees who live in a rented house can claim House Rent Allowance (HRA) exemption under the old tax regime. Employers usually ask for rent receipts as proof before giving the exemption in your salary TDS. Use our HRA calculator to see how much of your HRA is tax-free.

Rules to remember

  • Landlord PAN: if the rent you pay in a year is more than ₹1,00,000, your employer will ask for your landlord's PAN.
  • Revenue stamp: when rent above ₹5,000 is paid in cash, the receipt should carry a ₹1 revenue stamp signed across by the landlord.
  • New tax regime: HRA exemption is not available under the new regime, so receipts are useful only if you choose the old regime.
  • Keep proof of payment: paying rent by bank transfer or UPI gives stronger proof than cash.

How to use this rent receipt generator

  1. Fill your name, your landlord's name and PAN, the address and the monthly rent.
  2. Pick the first and last month. One receipt is made for each month.
  3. Press Generate, then Print or Save as PDF. Two receipts fit on each A4 page.
  4. Get each receipt signed by your landlord (and stamped for cash rent above ₹5,000), then submit to your employer.
