Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains how cooridorstore.in ("we", "us", "the website") handles information when you use the website. By using the website you agree to this policy.

1. Files and numbers you enter

Our calculators, reconciliation tools, invoice generator, Excel to Tally converter and Word to PDF tool run inside your browser. The values you type and the files you select (such as Excel, CSV or Word files) are processed on your own device. They are not uploaded to or stored on our server.

2. Information stored on your device

To make the tools easier to use, some settings are saved in your browser's local storage, for example your language and dark mode choice, business details in the invoice generator, ledger names and settings in the Tally tool, and your quiz best score. This information stays on your device. You can remove it anytime by clearing your browser's site data.

3. Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website, such as pages visited, time spent, device type and approximate location. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies. This information is aggregated and does not include the files or values you enter in our tools. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

4. Advertising

We may show advertisements served by Google AdSense and other third-party vendors.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

5. Cookies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some features, such as remembered settings, may not work without them.

6. Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and message. We use it only to reply to you and to improve the website, and we do not sell it. Please do not send PAN, Aadhaar, bank account numbers or other sensitive personal data by email.

7. Links to other websites

The website may link to government portals and other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

8. Children

The website is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 18.

9. Your rights

In line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you may ask us about personal data you have shared with us (such as by email), and ask for it to be corrected or deleted, by writing to the contact address below.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.

11. Contact and grievances

For any privacy question or grievance, email contact@cooridorstore.in.
