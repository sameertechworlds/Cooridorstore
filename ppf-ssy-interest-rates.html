Small Savings Interest Rates October-December 2026: PPF, SSY, SCSS, NSC

Small savings interest rates for October-December 2026: PPF 7.1%, SSY 8.2%, SCSS 8.2%, NSC 7.7%. Limits, lock-in, tax rules and maturity examples.

Updated 28 November 2026 · 7 min read · By the Cooridor Store team

Key takeaways

  • Rates are unchanged for October to December 2026: PPF 7.1%, SSY 8.2%, SCSS 8.2%, NSC 7.7%.
  • The next review is due around 31 December 2026 for January to March 2027.
  • ₹1.5 lakh a year in PPF for 15 years at 7.1% grows to about ₹40.7 lakh, tax-free.
  • 80C benefit on PPF, SSY, NSC and 5-year deposits is available only in the old regime.

Scheme by scheme

PPF

Best for long-term, tax-free savings. Partial withdrawal is allowed from the 7th year, and loans between the 3rd and 6th year. After 15 years you can extend in blocks of 5 years, with or without fresh deposits.

Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana

For a girl child below 10 years. Deposits are made for 15 years; the account matures at 21 years. Up to 50% can be withdrawn after she turns 18 for higher education. It currently offers the highest rate with tax-free maturity.

Senior Citizens Savings Scheme

For people aged 60 and above (55 for some retirees). Interest is paid every quarter, which makes it a popular source of regular income after retirement. TDS applies if interest exceeds ₹1 lakh a year.

NSC, KVP and Time Deposits

Fixed-return options with a sovereign guarantee. NSC interest is reinvested and counts for 80C in the old regime. KVP doubles your money in 115 months at the current rate.

How the rates are set

The government reviews small savings rates every quarter, linking them loosely to government bond yields of similar maturity. Rates for most schemes have stayed the same for many quarters. The next announcement, for January to March 2027, is due around 31 December 2026.

Frequently asked questions

What is the PPF interest rate for October-December 2026?

7.1% per year, unchanged from the previous quarter.

What is the Sukanya Samriddhi interest rate now?

8.2% per year for October to December 2026.

What is the SCSS interest rate?

8.2% per year, paid quarterly.

When will small savings rates change next?

The government reviews them every quarter. The rates for January to March 2027 are due around 31 December 2026.

Is PPF interest taxable?

No. PPF interest and maturity are tax-free. The 80C deduction on deposits is available only in the old regime.

How much will ₹1.5 lakh a year in PPF give after 15 years?

About ₹40.68 lakh at 7.1%, assuming the rate stays the same.
