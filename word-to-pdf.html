Word to PDF Converter

Convert .docx files to PDF in seconds. Your file stays on your device and is never uploaded.

In the print window, choose 'Save as PDF'. Complex layouts may look slightly different from Word.

How to convert Word to PDF

  1. Click the box or drag your .docx file into it.
  2. Check the preview on the right.
  3. Click Download PDF and choose Save as PDF.

Is it safe?

Yes. The conversion happens inside your browser. Your document is never sent to our servers.

Which files are supported?

Modern Word files (.docx) are supported. Older .doc files should first be saved as .docx in Word.
