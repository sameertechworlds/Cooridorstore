How to claim GST TCS deducted by Amazon, Flipkart and Meesho

By the Cooridor Store team · Published 17 November 2026 · 6 min read

In shortMarketplaces collect GST TCS at 0.5% of your net taxable sales and report it in GSTR-8. To use this money, accept the TCS on the GST portal under TDS and TCS credit received and file the statement. The amount then reaches your electronic cash ledger, and you can use it to pay GST.

What is GST TCS on e-commerce sales?

When you sell through Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra, JioMart or any other marketplace, the platform collects the money from the customer. Before paying you, it keeps back a small part as Tax Collected at Source (TCS) under Section 52 of the CGST Act, and deposits it with the government in your name.

Every e-commerce operator “shall collect an amount calculated at such rate not exceeding one per cent.”

— Section 52(1), CGST Act, 2017 (CBIC)

This is not an extra tax. It is an advance against your own GST, so you should always claim it back.

Current TCS rate

Type of saleTCS
Within your state0.25% CGST + 0.25% SGST
To another state0.5% IGST

The rate was reduced from 1% to 0.5% with effect from 10 July 2024. It is charged on the net taxable value: taxable sales minus taxable returns in the month, without GST.

Example

A seller in Uttar Pradesh sells goods worth ₹2,00,000 (taxable value) on Meesho in October, and customers return goods worth ₹20,000.

  • Net taxable value = ₹2,00,000 − ₹20,000 = ₹1,80,000
  • TCS at 0.5% = ₹900
  • If all sales were to other states, this is ₹900 IGST. If all were within Uttar Pradesh, it is ₹450 CGST + ₹450 SGST.

Meesho pays you ₹900 less and reports it in its GSTR-8 by 10 November. You can then claim the ₹900 on the GST portal.

Step-by-step: claim TCS on the GST portal

  1. Log in to the GST Portal.
  2. Go to Services → Returns → TDS and TCS credit received.
  3. Select the financial year and the month, and click Search.
  4. Click Prepare Online and open the TCS Credit Received tile. You will see one line for each platform with its GSTIN, the net value and the TCS.
  5. Compare each line with that platform's TCS report or your books. Select the matching lines and click Accept. Reject only lines that are wrong, and choose a reason.
  6. Check the Amendments tile too, in case a platform corrected an earlier month.
  7. Click Preview, then Proceed to file. Tick the declaration, choose the signatory and file with EVC (OTP) or DSC.
  8. After filing you get an ARN. The accepted TCS now appears in your electronic cash ledger.

Accepted credit is credited to the electronic cash ledger only after the TDS and TCS Credit Received statement is filed.

— GST Portal user manual (tutorial.gst.gov.in)

The portal does not let you file until you have acted on every line, so do not leave any line blank.

Keep the GST TCS ledger separate from the income tax TDS ledger. Platforms also deduct income tax TDS at 0.1% of gross sales, which you claim in your income tax return, not on the GST portal.

Common mistakes

  • Not filing after accepting. Accepting alone is not enough. Until you file, the money does not reach the cash ledger.
  • Rejecting because of small differences. Rounding or timing differences of a few rupees are normal. Reject only real errors.
  • Booking TCS as an expense. TCS is your money with the government, so it is an asset (receivable), not an expense.
  • Mixing GST TCS with income tax TDS. They go to different places and are claimed differently.
  • Never checking the amount. If TCS does not match 0.5% of your net sales on that platform, your sales or returns may be recorded wrongly somewhere.

Frequently asked questions

1. What is the GST TCS rate on e-commerce sales?

0.5% of the net taxable value of supplies made through the platform: 0.25% CGST plus 0.25% SGST for sales within your state, or 0.5% IGST for sales to other states. The rate was 1% before 10 July 2024.

2. Is TCS charged on the sale value including GST?

No. TCS is calculated on the net taxable value, that is, taxable sales minus taxable returns in the month, excluding GST.

3. Where can I see the TCS deducted by Amazon, Flipkart or Meesho?

On the GST portal under Services, Returns, TDS and TCS credit received, and in GSTR-2A. The platform also shows TCS in its payment or tax reports.

4. Do I need to accept TCS credit on the GST portal?

Yes. You must accept or reject every record and file the TDS and TCS Credit Received statement. Accepted credit reaches your electronic cash ledger only after you file it.

5. When should I reject a TCS entry?

Reject it only if the details are wrong, for example the amount does not match your sales on that platform. Rejected entries go back to the platform so it can correct them in its GSTR-8.

6. Can I use TCS credit to pay GST?

Yes. Once it is in the electronic cash ledger, you can use it to pay GST, interest, late fee or penalty, or claim a refund of the excess balance.

7. Is GST TCS the same as income tax TDS deducted by the platform?

No. GST TCS (0.5%) goes to your GST cash ledger. Income tax TDS (0.1% of gross sales) appears in Form 26AS and is claimed in your income tax return.

8. How do I record TCS in Tally or my books?

Debit a TCS Receivable ledger and credit the platform party when the settlement shows TCS. When you accept the credit and it reaches the cash ledger, debit the GST cash ledger and credit TCS Receivable.

9. What if the platform deducted TCS but it is not showing on the portal?

The platform may not have filed GSTR-8 for that month yet. GSTR-8 is due on the 10th of the next month. If it still does not appear, raise the issue with seller support.

10. How can I check that TCS matches my sales on each platform?

Use the free E-commerce Reconciliation tool. It works out expected TCS at 0.5% of net taxable sales and compares it with the settlement report, GSTR-8 and your books.
