How your JioMart payout is calculated

Net payout = Sale value − commission − shipping − other fees − GST on fees − TCS − TDS − refunds

Example: an order of ₹1,050 (₹1,000 + 5% GST) with ₹126 commission, ₹60 shipping, 18% GST of ₹33.48 on these fees, TCS of ₹5 (0.5% of ₹1,000) and TDS of ₹1.05 (0.1% of ₹1,050) gives a net payout of ₹824.47.

Frequently asked questions

1. How do I reconcile JioMart payments with my books?

Upload the JioMart order report, your sales register, the settlement report and your bank statement. The tool matches orders with your invoices, flags unpaid orders and links each payout to your bank.

2. Which JioMart reports do I need?

At least two: the JioMart orders or sales report and the sales register from your books. Add the JioMart payment or settlement report and your bank statement to check payouts, and your fee and TCS ledgers to check expenses and TCS.

3. Why is my JioMart payout less than my sales?

JioMart deducts commission, shipping or fulfilment fees, other charges, GST at 18% on these fees, TCS and TDS, and adjusts refunds for returns. The tool shows each deduction step by step from sale value to the amount paid.

4. How much GST TCS does JioMart deduct?

E-commerce operators collect TCS under Section 52 of the CGST Act at 0.5% of the net taxable value of supplies (0.25% CGST plus 0.25% SGST, or 0.5% IGST). The rate was reduced from 1% with effect from 10 July 2024.

5. How do I claim the TCS deducted by JioMart?

Log in to the GST portal and open Returns, TDS and TCS credit received. Accept the TCS shown for the month and file. The accepted amount is credited to your electronic cash ledger and can be used to pay GST.

6. What TDS does JioMart deduct on my payments?

E-commerce operators deduct income tax TDS at 0.1% of the gross amount of sales (earlier Section 194-O). It appears in your Form 26AS and AIS, and you can claim it in your income tax return.

7. How should I record JioMart commission and fees in my books?

Book commission, shipping and other fees as expenses against JioMart as the party, and the GST charged on them as input tax credit. JioMart issues tax invoices for its fees, so check that they appear in your GSTR-2B before claiming ITC.

8. What if an order is in the JioMart report but not in my books?

The sale was not recorded, or was recorded with a different order or invoice number. Record the missing invoice, or add the order ID to the existing entry, and run the reconciliation again.

9. How are returns and RTO handled?

In the orders report, rows with type Return, RTO, cancelled or refund are subtracted from the order value. In your books, credit notes with negative amounts reduce sales. If both sides record the return, the order shows as matched.

10. Is my data safe with this tool?

Yes. All files are read inside your browser and never uploaded to any server, so order, customer and bank data stays on your computer.
