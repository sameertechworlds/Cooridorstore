👑 Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi

Classic Indian chit game with 4 players: you and 3 friends. Raja 1000, Mantri 800, Sipahi 500, Chor 0. If you are the Mantri, find the Chor!

Press New game to start.

Scoreboard

How to play

Four chits are shuffled and each player gets one. The Raja reveals himself and orders the Mantri to find the Chor. The Mantri must guess which of the remaining two players is the Chor. If the guess is right, the Mantri keeps 800 points and the Chor gets 0. If wrong, the Mantri and Chor swap points. The Raja always gets 1000 and the Sipahi 500. After all rounds, the highest score wins.
