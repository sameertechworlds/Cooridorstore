👑 Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi

The classic Indian chit game on a virtual table. Four players, four chits: Raja 1000, Mantri 800, Sipahi 500, Chor 0. When you are the Mantri, catch the Chor!

Press New game to deal the chits.

How to play Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi

Four chits are shuffled and dealt, one to each player. The Raja announces himself and orders the Mantri to find the Chor. The Mantri points at one of the other two players. If the guess is right, the Mantri keeps 800 points and the Chor gets 0. If wrong, the Chor takes the Mantri's 800 points. The Raja always gets 1000 and the Sipahi 500. After the last round, the player with the highest total wins.
