What is HRA?

    
House Rent Allowance (HRA) is a part of salary paid by employers to help employees pay rent. Under Section 10(13A) of the Income Tax Act, a portion of HRA can be tax-free if you live in a rented house.

    
HRA exemption formula

    
The exempt HRA is the lowest of these three amounts:

    
1. Actual HRA received
2. Rent paid − 10% of (Basic + DA)
3. 50% of (Basic + DA) in metro cities, or 40% elsewhere

    
Metro cities for HRA are Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata and Chennai. The remaining HRA is added to your taxable salary.

    
Worked example

    
Basic + DA = ₹40,000 per month, HRA = ₹20,000, rent = ₹18,000, non-metro city.

    
  1. Actual HRA = ₹20,000
  2. Rent − 10% of basic = 18,000 − 4,000 = ₹14,000
  3. 40% of basic = ₹16,000

    
The lowest is ₹14,000 per month, so ₹1,68,000 a year is tax-free and ₹72,000 a year is taxable.

    
Documents you need

    
  • Rent receipts or rent agreement
  • Landlord's PAN if annual rent is above ₹1,00,000

    
Frequently asked questions

    
Can I claim HRA in the new tax regime?

    
No. HRA exemption is available only if you choose the old tax regime.

    
Can I pay rent to my parents and claim HRA?

    
Yes, if you genuinely pay rent and your parent owns the house. They must show the rent as income.
