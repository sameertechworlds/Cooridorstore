PDF to Word Converter

Turn a PDF into an editable Word file. Conversion happens in your browser, so your document is never uploaded.

Preview of extracted text

How to convert PDF to Word

  1. Choose your PDF or drag it into the box.
  2. Keep original line breaks on for invoices, statements and forms. Turn it off for letters and articles so lines join into paragraphs.
  3. Press Convert to Word, check the preview, and download the .docx file.

What gets converted

  • All text, page by page, with headings in bold based on font size.
  • Spacing between columns is kept with tabs, so simple tables stay readable.
  • Images, borders and exact fonts are not copied. You get clean, editable text.

Scanned PDFs

If your PDF is a scan or a photo, it contains pictures of text instead of real text, so nothing can be extracted. Such files need OCR (text recognition), which this tool does not do yet.

FAQ

Is it safe for confidential documents?

Yes. The PDF is read and the Word file is created inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Does it work with Hindi PDFs?

It depends on how the PDF was made. Many Hindi PDFs use fonts that store text in a non-standard way, so the extracted Hindi may look wrong.
