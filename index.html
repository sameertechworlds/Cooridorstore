Frequently asked questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes. All calculators, reconciliation tools, the invoice generator, Excel to Tally and PDF tools are free to use, with no signup and no watermark.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Excel, PDF and Word files are processed inside your browser and never leave your device. This makes the tools safe for client and bank data.

Are the tax rules up to date?

We follow the latest GST rates and income tax rules for India. Tax law changes often, so please confirm important decisions with a qualified professional.

Can I use the tools on my phone?

Yes. The calculators work on any phone. File tools work best on a laptop or desktop for large files.
