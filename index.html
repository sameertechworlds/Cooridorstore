What is Cooridor Store?

Cooridor Store is a free collection of online tools for GST, income tax, salary, accounting and PDF work in India. It is built for small business owners, accountants, CA firms, salaried employees and online sellers who need quick, correct answers without buying software or creating an account.

For GST, you can calculate GST at any rate, create a GST invoice, check a GSTIN, reconcile GSTR-2B with your purchase register, match GSTR-1 with e-invoices, work out late fees and draft a reply to notices such as ASMT-10, DRC-01, DRC-01B and REG-17. Online sellers can reconcile Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra and JioMart payouts and claim the GST TCS collected by marketplaces.

For income tax and salary, the income tax calculator compares the old and new regimes for FY 2026-27, the in-hand salary calculator follows the new Labour Code 50% wage rule and the ₹25,000 EPF wage ceiling, and there are HRA, 80GG, TDS, gratuity, EMI and SIP calculators with step-up, goal planning and real mutual fund backtests.

For accounting and documents, you can convert a bank statement PDF to Excel, reconcile a bank account, import invoices from Excel into Tally, and edit, merge, compress or convert PDF files. All tools are in English and Hindi, work on mobile, and run entirely in your browser, so your financial files stay private. Rates and rules are checked against official government sources.

Frequently asked questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes. Every tool on Cooridor Store is free to use, with no signup, no trial period and no watermark on downloads.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Bank statements, invoices, GST files and PDFs are processed inside your browser on your own device. They are not uploaded to our servers.

How do I calculate GST on an amount?

To add GST, multiply the amount by the GST rate and add it: ₹10,000 at 18% is ₹1,800 GST, total ₹11,800. To remove GST from an inclusive price, divide by (1 + rate): ₹11,800 ÷ 1.18 = ₹10,000. Within a state the GST splits equally into CGST and SGST; between states it is charged as IGST. Use the Quick GST box above.

What are the current GST rates in India?

Since the GST rate rationalisation of September 2025, most goods and services fall under 5% or 18%, with a 40% rate for luxury and sin goods. A special 3% rate applies to gold and silver, and some essentials are exempt. Check the rate for your HSN or SAC code on the GST portal.

Which tax regime is better, old or new?

For most salaried people without large deductions, the new regime is better in FY 2026-27, because taxable income up to ₹12 lakh is tax-free after the Section 87A rebate and the standard deduction is ₹75,000. The old regime can be better if your HRA, 80C, 80D and home loan deductions are large. Our income tax and in-hand salary calculators compare both.

How do I reconcile GSTR-2B with my purchase register?

Download GSTR-2B from the GST portal as Excel, export your purchase register from your accounting software, and upload both to the GSTR-2B Reconciliation tool. It matches invoices by GSTIN, invoice number, date and amount, and lists missing, mismatched and extra invoices so you claim only eligible ITC.

Can I convert a bank statement PDF to Excel?

Yes. The Bank Statement to Excel tool reads the PDF in your browser and gives date, narration, debit, credit and balance in clean Excel columns, ready for bank reconciliation or Tally import.

How do I import invoices from Excel into Tally?

Prepare your invoices in the given Excel format, upload them to the Invoices to Tally tool, map ledger names to your Tally chart of accounts, and download the XML. In Tally, use Import of Data › Vouchers to load the file.

Are the tax rules up to date?

Yes. The tools are updated for FY 2026-27, including the Income-tax Act 2025, the new Labour Code wage rules, the ₹25,000 EPF wage ceiling from 17 September 2026 and current GST rates. Each tool lists its official sources.

Can I use the tools on my phone and in Hindi?

Yes. Every tool works on mobile browsers, and you can switch between English and Hindi with the EN / हिं button at the top of any page.
