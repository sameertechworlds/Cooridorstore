How to convert a bank statement PDF to Excel

  1. Download your statement as a PDF from net banking. A downloaded statement works better than a scanned one.
  2. Choose the file above. If it asks for a password, enter the statement password (often a mix of your name and date of birth or customer ID, as set by your bank).
  3. Check the Balance check score. Rows in red did not match the running balance and should be checked against the PDF.
  4. Download the Excel. You can use it straight away in our bank reconciliation tool.

What makes this converter reliable

  • Running balance check: for every row we test that previous balance − withdrawal + deposit = new balance. A full score means no transaction was missed or misread.
  • Multi-line narration: narration that continues on the next line is joined to the right transaction.
  • Different layouts: separate Withdrawal/Deposit columns, a single Amount column with Dr/Cr, amounts marked Cr or Dr, and newest-first statements are all handled.
  • Private: the PDF is read inside your browser and never uploaded, which matters for bank data.

Limitations

  • Scanned or photographed statements contain images, not text, and need OCR.
  • Unusual layouts may not be read fully. The Raw Text sheet in the Excel still gives you every line of the PDF, split into cells.

FAQ

Which banks are supported?

The converter reads the column headings of the statement instead of relying on one bank's format, so it works with most Indian bank statements that list Date, Narration, Withdrawal, Deposit and Balance.

Is my bank statement safe?

Yes. Nothing is uploaded. The file and password stay in your browser.
