Last date to claim ITC for FY 2025-26: 30 November 2026

Input tax credit (ITC) for any invoice of FY 2025-26 must be claimed in a GSTR-3B filed by 30 November 2026, or by the date you file the annual return for that year, whichever comes first. This is the time limit in Section 16(4) of the CGST Act. Credit you miss now is usually lost for good, so this is the most expensive deadline of the GST year.

Who should check right now

  • Businesses whose suppliers filed GSTR-1 late, so invoices appeared in GSTR-2B after you closed the month.
  • Anyone who kept ITC on hold because goods arrived late or a debit note came in afterwards.
  • Businesses that reversed ITC under the 180-day rule and have since paid the supplier. Re-claim is allowed once payment is made.

15-minute checklist

  1. Download GSTR-2B for April 2025 to March 2026 and match it with your purchase register.
  2. List invoices that are in 2B but not yet claimed in any 3B.
  3. Drop blocked credits under Section 17(5), such as food, personal vehicles and gifts.
  4. Claim the rest in the GSTR-3B you file on or before 30 November 2026, in Table 4A(5) or the relevant row.
  5. Keep a working sheet. Officers ask for it when they send DRC-01C for ITC mismatches.
Remember: filing GSTR-9 before 30 November ends the window early. Claim pending ITC first, then file the annual return.

GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C for FY 2025-26: due 31 December 2026

GSTR-9 is the yearly summary of everything you reported in GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B. For FY 2025-26, both GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C are due on 31 December 2026. The portal lets you open GSTR-9 only after every GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B of the year is filed.

Aggregate turnover in FY 2025-26What to file
Up to ₹2 croreGSTR-9 is optional (exempt)
Above ₹2 crore up to ₹5 croreGSTR-9 mandatory
Above ₹5 croreGSTR-9 and self-certified GSTR-9C

Why this year is different

FY 2025-26 covers two rate structures. Rates changed under GST 2.0 on 22 September 2025, so the same item may appear at 12% before that date and 5% after it. Make sure your HSN summary and rate-wise outward supplies match the books on both sides of that date.

Mistakes that cost the most

  • Filing GSTR-9 before claiming pending ITC. Filing ends the ITC window for the year.
  • Reporting turnover that does not match the audited books. GSTR-9 cannot be revised once filed.
  • Ignoring differences between GSTR-1, e-invoices and e-way bills. These are the first things an officer compares.

Income-tax Act, 2025: what changed from 1 April 2026

The Income-tax Act, 2025 came into force on 1 April 2026 and replaced the Income-tax Act, 1961. It is mostly a rewrite in simpler language. Tax rates and slabs for FY 2026-27 did not change because of it.

The four changes most people notice

  1. "Tax Year" replaces "previous year" and "assessment year". Income earned from April 2026 to March 2027 is simply Tax Year 2026-27.
  2. New section numbers. For example, almost all non-salary TDS now sits in one section, 393, with a table, instead of 194A, 194C, 194J and so on. Old numbers still appear in older articles and software.
  3. New Income-tax Rules, 2026 and new forms. Several forms were merged or renumbered, so check the form name on the portal before filing.
  4. Same slabs. The new regime remains the default, with zero tax on income up to ₹12 lakh after the rebate.

Which law applies to your return?

Income earnedLawReturn filed in
Up to 31 March 2026 (FY 2025-26)Income-tax Act, 19612026
From 1 April 2026 (Tax Year 2026-27)Income-tax Act, 20252027

The Income Tax Department has published a utility that maps old section numbers to new ones. Use it when an old notice or a payroll setting refers to a 1961 section.

Advance tax: next instalment due 15 December 2026

If your tax for the year, after TDS, is ₹10,000 or more, you must pay it in advance in instalments. Senior citizens with no business income are exempt.

Due datePay at least
15 June15% of the year's tax
15 September45%
15 December75%
15 March100%

People under presumptive taxation (sections 44AD and 44ADA under the old numbering) can pay the whole amount by 15 March.

Interest if you miss

  • Late instalment: 1% per month on the shortfall, usually for 3 months (1 month for the March instalment).
  • Paid less than 90% by 31 March: a further 1% per month until you pay.

Who it catches most often: freelancers, landlords, traders, and salaried people with big FD interest or capital gains on which no TDS was cut. Pay online through the e-Pay Tax option on the e-filing portal using challan ITNS 280.

How to reply to a GST notice: ASMT-10, DRC-01B, DRC-01C

Most GST notices are system mismatches, not fraud cases. A clear, point-wise reply with working papers closes the majority of them. Not replying is the costly option: unanswered DRC-01B and DRC-01C notices can block your next GSTR-1 under Rule 59(6).

NoticeWhy it comesReply in
ASMT-10Scrutiny of returns finds differencesASMT-11, within 30 days
DRC-01BTax in GSTR-1 is higher than tax paid in GSTR-3BPart B, within 7 days
DRC-01CITC in GSTR-3B is higher than GSTR-2BPart B, within 7 days
REG-17 / REG-31Proposal to cancel or suspend registrationREG-18, within the time given

A reply structure that works

  1. Quote the notice number, date, GSTIN and tax period.
  2. Take each point separately: the amount, the reason for the difference, and the proof.
  3. Attach a reconciliation sheet, for example GSTR-1 vs 3B by month, or 2B vs books by invoice.
  4. Where tax is genuinely short, pay it with interest through DRC-03 and mention the payment reference.
  5. Ask for a personal hearing if you need to explain further.

For demand notices for FY 2024-25 onwards, Section 74A applies. Paying tax and interest early can reduce or remove the penalty, so act quickly.

New Labour Code: what changes in your salary and PF

The four Labour Codes change how salary is structured. Three changes matter most for take-home pay.

1. Wages must be at least 50% of pay

Basic pay, DA and retaining allowance together must be at least half of total pay. If allowances are more than 50%, the excess is added back to "wages". Because PF and gratuity are calculated on wages, a higher wage base usually means more PF and gratuity and slightly lower monthly in-hand pay.

2. PF wage ceiling raised to ₹25,000

The EPF wage ceiling went up from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 a month from 17 September 2026. For employers who cap PF at the ceiling, the maximum employee PF rises from ₹1,800 to ₹3,000 a month. Your retirement corpus grows faster, and take-home falls by up to ₹1,200 a month.

3. Gratuity for fixed-term employees after one year

Fixed-term employees become eligible for gratuity after one year of service instead of five. Regular employees still need five years, except in cases such as death or disability.

Example: CTC ₹60,000 a month with basic ₹20,000. Under the 50% rule, wages become ₹30,000. With PF capped at ₹25,000, employee PF moves from ₹1,800 to ₹3,000. Check your own numbers in the salary calculator.

SIP vs lumpsum, and how much SIP you need for ₹1 crore

A SIP invests a fixed amount every month, so you buy more units when prices fall and fewer when they rise. A lumpsum puts the whole amount in at once. Over long periods a lumpsum often does better in a rising market, but a SIP spreads the risk of bad timing and fits a monthly salary.

Monthly SIP for ₹1 crore (assuming 12% a year)

TimeMonthly SIP
10 years₹43,041
15 years₹19,819
20 years₹10,009
25 years₹5,270
30 years₹2,833

Starting 5 years earlier roughly halves the monthly amount. Raising your SIP by 10% every year (step-up) cuts the starting amount a lot more.

Tax on equity mutual funds

  • Held more than 12 months: 12.5% on gains above ₹1.25 lakh a year.
  • Held 12 months or less: 20%.
  • Each SIP instalment has its own 12-month clock.

Returns are not guaranteed. The 12% figure is an assumption for planning, not a promise. Check a fund's scheme documents and risk level, and use only SEBI-registered advisers for personal advice.

Bank reconciliation and Tally import: a monthly routine

A bank reconciliation statement (BRS) explains why your cash book balance and your bank statement balance do not match. Doing it every month catches missed entries, duplicate payments and fraud early, and makes year-end closing far quicker.

BRS in five steps

  1. Convert the bank statement PDF to Excel so you can sort and match it.
  2. Match entries by amount and date with the bank ledger in your books.
  3. List what is only in the books: cheques issued but not presented, deposits not yet credited.
  4. List what is only in the bank: charges, interest, direct receipts, auto-debits. Post these in your books.
  5. Start from the books balance, adjust for the timing items, and confirm you reach the bank balance.

Then import, don't retype

If invoices are kept in Excel, convert them to a Tally XML file and import them in one go through Gateway of Tally, Import, Transactions. Check that ledger names in the sheet exactly match the ledgers in Tally, including GST ledgers, or the import will create new ledgers or fail.

Monthly routine: statement to Excel, BRS, GSTR-2B reconciliation, invoice import. About an hour for a small business, and your GST and income tax filings become much easier.

Frequently asked questions

What is the last date to claim ITC for FY 2025-26?

The time limit under Section 16(4) is 30 November 2026, or the date you file the annual return for FY 2025-26, whichever is earlier. Claim pending credit in the GSTR-3B filed by that date.

Can I still file my income tax return for FY 2025-26?

Yes. A belated return for AY 2026-27 can be filed until 31 December 2026 with a late fee of ₹1,000 if total income is up to ₹5 lakh, or ₹5,000 above that, plus interest on any unpaid tax.

When is GSTR-9 due for FY 2025-26?

GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C for FY 2025-26 are due on 31 December 2026. GSTR-9 is optional up to ₹2 crore turnover, and GSTR-9C applies above ₹5 crore.

Is income up to ₹12 lakh tax-free in FY 2026-27?

Under the new regime, a rebate makes tax nil on taxable income up to ₹12 lakh. Salaried people also get a ₹75,000 standard deduction, so a salary of up to ₹12.75 lakh pays no tax.

What is the EPF interest rate for 2025-26?

EPFO credited interest at 8.25% for 2025-26, the same rate as the two previous years.

Which cities get 50% HRA exemption from April 2026?

Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Ahmedabad. All other cities get 40%. HRA exemption is available only in the old regime.

What are the current GST slabs?

Since 22 September 2025 the main rates are 5% and 18%, with a 40% rate for luxury and sin goods, nil for essentials and 3% for gold and silver.
