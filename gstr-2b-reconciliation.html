What is GSTR-2B reconciliation?

GSTR-2B is an auto-drafted, static statement of input tax credit (ITC) generated every month on the GST portal from your suppliers' GSTR-1 filings. GSTR-2B reconciliation means comparing this statement with your purchase register to make sure you claim only eligible ITC in GSTR-3B, and that no credit is missed.

Why is ITC reconciliation important?

  • ITC can be claimed only if the invoice appears in your GSTR-2B.
  • Claiming excess ITC can lead to notices, interest and penalty.
  • Missing invoices mean lost credit and higher tax outflow.
  • It helps you follow up with suppliers who have not filed GSTR-1.

How to reconcile GSTR-2B with purchase register in Excel

  1. Download GSTR-2B in Excel from the GST portal: Returns → GSTR-2B → Download Excel.
  2. Export your purchase register from Tally, Busy or any software in Excel.
  3. Upload both files above. The tool auto-detects columns like GSTIN, invoice number, taxable value, IGST, CGST and SGST. Change them if needed.
  4. Click Reconcile now and review the four categories.
  5. Download the Excel report and follow up on mismatches.

How does the matching work?

Invoices are matched on supplier GSTIN and invoice number. The tool cleans invoice numbers by removing spaces, slashes, hyphens and leading zeros, so "INV/001" and "inv-1" are treated as the same bill. Differences within your tolerance (default ₹1) count as matched.

Matched: same GSTIN + invoice, difference ≤ tolerance
Mismatch: same GSTIN + invoice, difference > tolerance
In books only: ITC at risk, follow up with supplier
In 2B only: possibly unrecorded purchase

Frequently asked questions

1. What is GSTR-2B?

GSTR-2B is a monthly statement of input tax credit (ITC) prepared by the GST portal from the invoices your suppliers report in GSTR-1 or IFF. Unlike GSTR-2A, it does not change once generated.

2. Why should I reconcile GSTR-2B with my purchase register?

ITC can be claimed in GSTR-3B only for invoices that appear in GSTR-2B. Reconciling every month helps you avoid excess ITC claims, interest and notices, and shows which suppliers have not reported your invoices.

3. How do I download GSTR-2B in Excel?

On the GST portal, go to Services, Returns, Returns Dashboard. Select the financial year and month, open GSTR-2B and click Download Excel.

4. How does this tool match invoices?

It compares the supplier GSTIN, invoice number and tax amounts in both files, then marks each invoice as matched, mismatched, missing in 2B or missing in books. You can download the full result in Excel.

5. What if an invoice is in my books but not in GSTR-2B?

The supplier has usually not filed or reported that invoice. Follow up with the supplier and do not claim the ITC until it appears in GSTR-2B.

6. What if an invoice is in GSTR-2B but not in my books?

Check whether it is a genuine purchase that was not recorded. If it is, enter it in your books. If not, ask the supplier, as they may have used your GSTIN by mistake.

7. What is the difference between GSTR-2A and GSTR-2B?

GSTR-2A is dynamic and keeps updating whenever suppliers file. GSTR-2B is fixed for each month, so it is the basis for claiming ITC in GSTR-3B.

8. When is GSTR-2B generated?

It is generated on the 14th of the following month. With the Invoice Management System (IMS), you can accept or reject invoices and get GSTR-2B computed again before filing GSTR-3B.

9. How often should I reconcile GSTR-2B?

Every month, before filing GSTR-3B. Monthly reconciliation catches missing invoices early, while there is still time to ask suppliers to correct them.

10. Are my files safe with this tool?

Yes. Both Excel files are processed inside your browser and never leave your computer, so client data stays private.
