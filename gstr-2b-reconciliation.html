What is GSTR-2B reconciliation?

GSTR-2B is an auto-drafted, static statement of input tax credit (ITC) generated every month on the GST portal from your suppliers' GSTR-1 filings. GSTR-2B reconciliation means comparing this statement with your purchase register to make sure you claim only eligible ITC in GSTR-3B, and that no credit is missed.

Why is ITC reconciliation important?

  • ITC can be claimed only if the invoice appears in your GSTR-2B.
  • Claiming excess ITC can lead to notices, interest and penalty.
  • Missing invoices mean lost credit and higher tax outflow.
  • It helps you follow up with suppliers who have not filed GSTR-1.

How to reconcile GSTR-2B with purchase register in Excel

  1. Download GSTR-2B in Excel from the GST portal: Returns → GSTR-2B → Download Excel.
  2. Export your purchase register from Tally, Busy or any software in Excel.
  3. Upload both files above. The tool auto-detects columns like GSTIN, invoice number, taxable value, IGST, CGST and SGST. Change them if needed.
  4. Click Reconcile now and review the four categories.
  5. Download the Excel report and follow up on mismatches.

How does the matching work?

Invoices are matched on supplier GSTIN and invoice number. The tool cleans invoice numbers by removing spaces, slashes, hyphens and leading zeros, so "INV/001" and "inv-1" are treated as the same bill. Differences within your tolerance (default ₹1) count as matched.

Matched: same GSTIN + invoice, difference ≤ tolerance
Mismatch: same GSTIN + invoice, difference > tolerance
In books only: ITC at risk, follow up with supplier
In 2B only: possibly unrecorded purchase

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between GSTR-2A and GSTR-2B?

GSTR-2A is dynamic and keeps changing as suppliers file returns. GSTR-2B is static for each month and is the basis for claiming ITC in GSTR-3B.

Can I use this for GSTR-2A reconciliation?

Yes. Upload the GSTR-2A Excel in place of 2B; the matching works the same way.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Files are read inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server.
