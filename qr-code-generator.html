Frequently asked questions

1. How do I make a UPI QR code?

Choose UPI, enter your UPI ID and name, add an amount if you want a fixed payment, and download the PNG. Customers can scan it with any UPI app.

2. Is a UPI QR code made here safe?

Yes. Money goes only to the UPI ID you enter, and the QR is created inside your browser. Always check the UPI ID before printing or sharing it.

3. Can I set a fixed amount in the UPI QR code?

Yes. Enter the amount and it fills in automatically when scanned. Leave it blank if the customer should type the amount.

4. Which apps can scan this UPI QR code?

Any UPI app, including Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and BHIM, as well as bank UPI apps.

5. Do these QR codes expire?

No. They are static QR codes and work as long as the UPI ID, link, phone number or WiFi details stay the same.

6. Can I print the QR code on invoices or a shop counter?

Yes. Download the PNG in large size for printing. Keep enough white space around the code and test-scan the printout.

7. How do I make a WhatsApp QR code?

Choose WhatsApp, enter the mobile number and an optional message. Scanning it opens a WhatsApp chat with that number.

8. How does a WiFi QR code work?

Enter the network name, password and security type. Scanning it with a phone camera connects to the WiFi without typing the password.

9. Is this QR code generator free?

Yes. It is free, unlimited and has no watermark or signup.

10. Is my data stored anywhere?

No. The QR code is made inside your browser and nothing you type is sent to any server.
