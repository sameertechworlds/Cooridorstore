GST late fee rules for GSTR-3B and GSTR-1

  
Under Section 47 of the CGST Act, a late fee is charged for every day a return is filed after its due date. The reduced rates notified in June 2021 apply to GSTR-3B and GSTR-1:

  

   
CaseLate fee per dayMaximum late fee per return

   
Nil return₹20 (₹10 CGST + ₹10 SGST)₹500

   
Turnover up to ₹1.5 crore₹50 (₹25 + ₹25)₹2,000

   
Turnover ₹1.5 crore – ₹5 crore₹50 (₹25 + ₹25)₹5,000

   
Turnover above ₹5 crore₹50 (₹25 + ₹25)₹10,000

  

  
Interest on late payment of GST

  
Under Section 50, interest at 18% per year is charged on tax paid late. It applies only to the part of tax paid in cash (after using input tax credit), from the day after the due date until the date of payment. Unlike the late fee, interest has no maximum limit.

  
Interest = Cash tax × 18% × Days late ÷ 365

  
Example

  
GSTR-3B for October 2026 was due on 20 November 2026 and filed on 10 December 2026, which is 20 days late. Turnover is ₹1 crore and cash tax is ₹50,000.

  
    
   
  • Late fee = 20 × ₹50 = ₹1,000 (below the ₹2,000 cap), so ₹500 CGST + ₹500 SGST
    • 
   
  • Interest = ₹50,000 × 18% × 20 ÷ 365 = ₹493
    • 
   
  • Total = ₹1,493
    • 
  

  
Important points

  
    
   
  • Late fee must be paid in cash. Input tax credit cannot be used for it.
    • 
   
  • Late fee is charged per return. If both GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B are late, both attract a fee.
    • 
   
  • Returns cannot be filed after three years from their due date, so do not delay old returns.
