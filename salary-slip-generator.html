What is a salary slip?

A salary slip, also called a payslip or pay stub, is a monthly statement an employer gives an employee. It shows what the employee earned, what was deducted, and the net pay credited to the bank. Employers are expected to issue wage slips under Indian labour laws, and employees need them for loans, credit cards, visas, rented homes, job changes and income tax returns.

What are the common salary components?

Basic salary and DA

The fixed core of pay. Under the new Labour Codes, basic plus DA should be at least 50% of total pay. PF and gratuity are calculated on it.

House Rent Allowance (HRA)

Paid for rent. In the old tax regime part of it can be tax-free; from April 2026, eight cities get the higher 50% limit.

Special allowance

A flexible, fully taxable balancing component that makes up the rest of the gross salary.

Bonus, overtime and incentives

Variable pay added in the month it is paid. Add them as extra earning rows.

Provident Fund (PF)

12% of basic plus DA. Many employers cap it at the PF wage ceiling, which rose from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 a month from 17 September 2026, so the maximum employee PF is now ₹3,000 a month.

ESI

Employees earning ₹21,000 a month or less pay 0.75% of gross wages; the employer pays 3.25%.

Professional tax

A state tax of up to ₹2,500 a year, usually ₹200 a month with ₹300 in February in some states. Delhi, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan and Uttarakhand do not charge it.

Income tax (TDS)

Tax the employer deducts each month based on your expected annual tax. Use the income tax calculator to estimate it.

How are LOP days calculated?

Loss of pay days are working days for which no salary is paid, such as unpaid leave. When "Reduce earnings for LOP days" is on, each earning is multiplied by paid days and divided by working days. For example, with a ₹40,000 gross, 30 working days and 2 LOP days, earnings become ₹40,000 × 28 ÷ 30 = ₹37,333.33. PF and ESI are then worked out on the reduced amounts.

How is PF calculated on a salary slip?

Employee PF is 12% of basic plus DA. If PF is capped, it is 12% of basic up to ₹25,000 a month (₹15,000 before 17 September 2026). You can also choose 12% of full basic or no PF.

When is ESI deducted?

When gross wages are ₹21,000 a month or less. The employee pays 0.75% and the employer 3.25%.

What is the difference between a salary slip and Form 16?

A salary slip is a monthly statement of pay and deductions. Form 16 is an annual TDS certificate from the employer showing salary paid and tax deducted for the year, used to file the income tax return.

Is a salary slip valid without a signature?
