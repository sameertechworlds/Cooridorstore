What is e-invoice reconciliation?

Under GST, businesses above the notified turnover limit must report every B2B invoice, credit note and debit note to the Invoice Registration Portal (IRP) to get an IRN. These e-invoices auto-populate in GSTR-1. E-invoice reconciliation means comparing this portal data with your sales register to make sure every B2B invoice has a valid IRN and that the values match before filing GSTR-1.

Why reconcile GSTR-1 with e-invoices?

  • An invoice without a valid IRN is not treated as a valid tax invoice, and your customer may lose ITC.
  • Missed e-invoices lead to differences between GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and your customer's GSTR-2B.
  • Value or GSTIN mismatches cause notices and customer disputes.
  • For taxpayers with AATO of ₹10 crore or more, IRN must be generated within 30 days of the document date.

How to download e-invoice data

  1. Log in to the GST portal and open the Returns Dashboard for the period.
  2. In GSTR-1, use the e-Invoice download option to get the Excel of auto-populated e-invoices. You can also download IRN reports from the e-invoice (IRP) portal.
  3. From Tally, export the Sales Register: Display More Reports → Account Books → Sales Register → select period → Export (Ctrl + E / Alt + E) → Excel.

Menu names can differ slightly on the portals. Any Excel with document number, date, GSTIN and values will work.

How this tool matches invoices

  1. Documents are matched on document type (Invoice, Credit Note, Debit Note) and document number. Numbers are cleaned, so "INV/001" and "inv-1" match.
  2. After matching, GSTIN, date, taxable value and tax are compared. Every difference is listed by field.
  3. Invoices in books without a GSTIN are treated as B2C and kept separate, since e-invoicing applies to B2B supplies.
  4. Cancelled IRNs (from a status column) and IRNs generated after the time limit are flagged.

Frequently asked questions

Who needs to generate e-invoices?

Registered businesses whose aggregate turnover in any financial year since 2017-18 exceeds ₹5 crore must generate e-invoices for B2B supplies, exports and credit or debit notes. Check the latest notification for exemptions.

Can I fix a missed e-invoice?

Generate the IRN as soon as possible within the allowed time. If the time limit has passed, consult your tax advisor about the correct course of action.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Everything runs inside your browser. No file is uploaded.
