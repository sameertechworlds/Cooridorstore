This online PDF editor lets you change a PDF without installing software. You can type new text anywhere, change existing text, sign documents, fill forms with ticks and dates, highlight important lines, hide information with white out, add a logo or stamp, and rotate, reorder, delete or add pages. Everything runs in your browser, so your file stays on your device.

How do I edit a PDF online?

  1. Click Select PDF and choose your file, or drag it onto the box.
  2. Pick a tool from the toolbar, such as Text, Sign or White out, and click or drag on the page.
  3. Click any item to select it, drag to move it, and use the blue handle to resize. Press Delete to remove it.
  4. Use the page thumbnails to rotate, move or delete pages, or add a blank page.
  5. Click Download PDF to save the edited file.

Is it safe to edit a PDF here?

Yes. Your PDF is opened with PDF.js and saved with pdf-lib, two open-source libraries that run inside your browser. The file is never sent to our server, so it is suitable for bank statements, invoices, GST documents and other private papers. Password-protected PDFs must be unlocked before editing.

Frequently asked questions

1. Is this PDF editor really free?

Yes. All tools are free, with no signup and no watermark.

2. Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF is opened and edited in your browser, and the edited file is created on your device.

3. Can I edit existing text in a PDF?

Yes. Choose Edit text and click a line. The old text is covered with white and an editable text box with the same words appears on top. The new text uses a standard font, so it may look slightly different from the original.

4. How do I sign a PDF online?

Click Sign, draw your signature with a mouse or finger, or upload a photo of it, and place it on the page. Resize it with the blue handle and download.

5. Can I type in Hindi or use the ₹ symbol?

Yes. Hindi text and symbols such as ₹ are added as high-resolution images so they display correctly everywhere. Plain English text is added as real, selectable text.

6. Does it work on mobile?

Yes. It works in modern mobile browsers, and you can draw or sign with your finger.

Which technology powers this editor?

The editor saves your file with pdf-lib, a library built to "create and modify PDF documents in any JavaScript environment".

— pdf-lib project documentation
  • PDF.js by Mozilla: displays the PDF pages and reads existing text
  • pdf-lib: writes your changes into the PDF
  • ISO 32000-2: the international standard for the PDF format
