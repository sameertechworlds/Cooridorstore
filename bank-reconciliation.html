What is bank reconciliation?

Bank reconciliation is the process of comparing the bank account in your books (the bank ledger in Tally, Busy or any accounting software) with the bank statement, and explaining every difference. The result is a Bank Reconciliation Statement (BRS) that proves both balances agree.

Why do differences arise?

  • Cheques issued but not presented: recorded as payment in books, not yet debited by bank.
  • Cheques deposited but not credited: recorded as receipt in books, not yet credited by bank.
  • Direct credits: NEFT/UPI receipts, interest credited by bank, not yet entered in books.
  • Bank charges and direct debits: SMS charges, EMI, auto-debits not yet entered in books.
  • Errors: wrong amount, duplicate or missing entries.

BRS format

Balance as per books
+ Cheques issued but not presented
− Cheques deposited but not credited
+ Credits in bank not recorded in books
− Debits in bank not recorded in books
= Balance as per bank statement

How this tool matches entries

  1. Direction: a receipt in books (Debit) is matched only with a deposit in bank, and a payment (Credit) only with a withdrawal.
  2. Amount: must be equal, within your tolerance.
  3. Date: must fall within the date window. Closer dates score higher.
  4. Party name: words from the ledger particulars are searched in the bank narration, ignoring words like NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, UPI and TRANSFER. Truncated names like "SHARMA TRAD" are recognised.
  5. Cheque / reference number: if found on both sides, the match is treated as the strongest.
  6. Best match first: every possible pair gets a score and the highest-scoring pairs are matched first, so when the same amount appears many times, the right party is chosen.
  7. Amount mismatch: unmatched entries with the same party and close dates but different amounts are shown separately with the difference.

Each match shows a confidence level (High, Medium or Low) and the reason, so you can review Low-confidence matches quickly.

How to export the bank ledger from Tally

  1. Gateway of Tally → Display More Reports → Account Books → Ledger → select your bank ledger.
  2. Set the period (Alt + F2).
  3. Press Alt + E (Export), choose Excel format and export.

For the bank statement, download it from net banking in Excel or CSV format.

Frequently asked questions

Which bank statements are supported?

Any bank statement in Excel or CSV with date, narration, withdrawal and deposit columns, including SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, PNB and others. Columns are detected automatically and can be changed.

What if my statement has one amount column with Dr/Cr?

Map the amount column to Deposit and leave Withdrawal as none. Negative amounts are treated as withdrawals.

Is my bank data safe?

Yes. Everything runs inside your browser. No file is uploaded to any server.
