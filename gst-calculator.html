What is GST?

    
Goods and Services Tax (GST) is an indirect tax charged on the supply of goods and services in India. It replaced older taxes like VAT, excise and service tax. The seller collects GST from the buyer and deposits it with the government.


    
GST formulas

    
1. Adding GST (price is exclusive of GST)

    
GST amount = Original price × GST rate ÷ 100
Total price = Original price + GST amount

    
2. Removing GST (price is inclusive of GST)

    
Base price = Total price × 100 ÷ (100 + GST rate)
GST amount = Total price − Base price


    
Worked examples

    
Example 1: Add 18% GST to ₹10,000

    
GST = 10,000 × 18 ÷ 100 = ₹1,800. Total = 10,000 + 1,800 = ₹11,800. If the sale is within the same state, CGST = ₹900 and SGST = ₹900.

    
Example 2: Remove 18% GST from ₹11,800

    
Base price = 11,800 × 100 ÷ 118 = ₹10,000. GST = 11,800 − 10,000 = ₹1,800.

    
Example 3: Inter-state sale of ₹50,000 at 12%

    
GST = 50,000 × 12 ÷ 100 = ₹6,000, charged fully as IGST. Total = ₹56,000.

Common GST rates in India

    
Under the revised GST structure, most goods fall under 5% or 18%, while a 40% rate applies to select luxury and sin goods. Some items such as gold use 3%, and older invoices may still show 12% or 28%. Always confirm the current rate for your HSN or SAC code on the official GST portal.


    
Frequently asked questions

    
How do I calculate GST on a GST-inclusive price?

    
Divide the total by (100 + rate) and multiply by 100 to get the base price. The difference is the GST.
