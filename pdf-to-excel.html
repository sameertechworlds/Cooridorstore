How to convert PDF to Excel

  1. Drop one or more PDFs in the box. Password-protected files ask for their password.
  2. Choose Full tables for statements, registers and reports, or Pick fields to get one row per PDF with only the details you need.
  3. Click Convert, then set Your format: untick columns you do not want, rename them and change the order.
  4. Check the totals at the bottom of the preview against the PDF, fix any cell by clicking it, and download Excel or CSV.

Why the output is exact

Most digital PDFs, such as bank statements, GST reports, Tally exports and system-generated invoices, store their text inside the file. This tool reads that text directly, so every character and digit in Excel is the same as in the PDF. Nothing is retyped or guessed. The tool then rebuilds the table by finding the empty vertical gaps between columns across all pages, removes repeated page headers and footers, and joins descriptions that wrap onto a second line.

Scanned PDFs are pictures of paper with no text inside. They need OCR (optical character recognition) first, which can never be fully exact, so the tool warns you instead of guessing.

Your own format

Every office wants its Excel in a particular layout. With Your format you keep only the columns you need, in your order and with your column names. Save the format once, and the next time you convert similar PDFs it is applied automatically by matching column names.

With Pick fields, you list the details you want, such as Invoice No, Invoice Date, Seller GSTIN, Buyer GSTIN, Taxable Value, IGST and Grand Total. The tool looks for each label in every PDF and takes the value next to it or just below it. Fields of type GSTIN, PAN, date or amount are checked, so a wrong value is not picked up by mistake. Drop 100 invoices and get one clean Excel row per invoice.

Invoices to Excel, item by item

Choose Invoice items and drop all your invoices together. The Excel shows the first invoice number with its date and GSTINs, then every item of that invoice with HSN, quantity, rate, taxable value, GST % and GST amount, then an invoice total row, and then the next invoice. The Check column adds up the items and compares them with the total printed on the invoice, so you can see at once if anything is missing. Pick Flat layout to repeat the invoice number on every item row for pivot tables.

Use your own Excel format

In Pick fields, click Upload my Excel format and choose an Excel file that has only your column names, such as Sr No, Bill No, Bill Date, Supplier GSTIN, Description, HSN, Qty, Rate, Taxable Value, GST Amount and Total. The tool reads the names, works out what each column needs, and after conversion gives back the same Excel filled with data from every PDF.

Frequently asked questions

1. How accurate is this PDF to Excel converter?

For digital PDFs, the text and numbers are copied exactly as they are stored in the PDF, not retyped or guessed, so every character matches. The tool also rebuilds rows and columns, and shows totals of number columns so you can check them against the PDF before downloading.

2. Can I convert many PDFs at once?

Yes. Select or drop as many PDFs as you like. You can combine all of them into one Excel sheet with a File column, or get one sheet per PDF.

3. Can I get only the columns I need?

Yes. In Your format, untick columns you do not want, rename them and change their order. Save the format and it is applied automatically the next time.

4. Can it pick specific details like invoice number and total from each PDF?

Yes. Use Pick fields mode. Tell the tool which labels to look for, such as Invoice No or Grand Total, and it gives one Excel row per PDF with just those fields. A ready preset for GST invoices is included.

5. Does it work with scanned PDFs?

No. Scanned PDFs are images with no text inside, so they need OCR first. The tool tells you when a PDF has no text layer.

6. Can I open password-protected PDFs?

Yes. If a PDF has a password, the tool asks for it. The password is used only in your browser.

7. Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens inside your browser, so bank statements, invoices and client files never leave your computer.

8. Are numbers converted so Excel can add them?

Yes. Amounts like 1,23,456.78 or (500) become real numbers. Long numbers such as account, reference or phone numbers are kept as text so their digits are not changed.

9. What if a cell is wrong?

Click any cell in the preview to correct it before downloading. Your changes go into the Excel file.

10. Is this PDF to Excel tool free?

Yes. It is free, with no signup, no watermark and no limit on the number of files.
