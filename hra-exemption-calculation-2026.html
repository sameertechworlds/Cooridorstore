HRA Exemption 2026: How to Calculate, 8 Metro Cities and Rules

HRA exemption from April 2026: Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Ahmedabad now get 50%. Formula, worked examples, documents needed and 80GG if you get no HRA.

Updated 28 November 2026 · 7 min read · By the Cooridor Store team

Key takeaways

  • From 1 April 2026, eight cities get the 50% HRA limit: Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Ahmedabad.
  • Exempt HRA is the lowest of actual HRA, rent minus 10% of salary, and 50% (or 40%) of salary.
  • HRA exemption is available only in the old tax regime.
  • No HRA in salary? Claim rent under 80GG, up to ₹5,000 a month.

What changed from April 2026

The Income-tax Rules, 2026, which came into force with the new Income-tax Act on 1 April 2026, expanded the list of cities that get the higher HRA limit. Earlier only four cities qualified. Now eight cities get 50% of salary:

CityUntil FY 2025-26From FY 2026-27
Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai50%50%
Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad40%50%
All other cities40%40%

The HRA exemption formula

The tax-free part of your HRA is the lowest of these three amounts, worked out for the period you paid rent:

  1. HRA actually received from your employer.
  2. Rent paid minus 10% of salary.
  3. 50% of salary if you live in one of the eight cities, otherwise 40%.

"Salary" means basic pay plus dearness allowance (if it counts for retirement benefits) plus commission based on a fixed percentage of turnover. The rest of the HRA is taxable.

In the Hyderabad example, the old 40% limit would have capped the exemption at ₹2,40,000. Under the new rules ₹60,000 more is tax-free, which saves up to ₹18,720 for someone in the 30% bracket (with cess).

Documents and rules to follow

  • Rent receipts for every month, signed by the landlord, and ideally a rent agreement.
  • Landlord's PAN if annual rent is more than ₹1 lakh. Without it, your employer may not allow the exemption.
  • Paying rent to parents is allowed if they own the house, the payment is real (bank transfer), and they show it as income in their return. Rent to a spouse is generally not accepted.
  • If your employer did not give you the exemption, you can still claim it when you file your return, as long as you choose the old regime and have the proof.

Is HRA exempt in the new regime?

No. In the new regime the full HRA is taxable. That is why people who pay high rent in a big city often still find the old regime cheaper. Compare both with your actual numbers.

No HRA in your salary? Use 80GG

If you are self-employed or your salary has no HRA, you can claim rent under 80GG (the old section number) in the old regime. The deduction is the lowest of:

  • ₹5,000 a month (₹60,000 a year),
  • 25% of total income, or
  • rent paid minus 10% of total income.

You, your spouse or minor child must not own a house in the city where you live and work. You file a declaration in Form 10BA with the return.

Frequently asked questions

Which cities are metro for HRA from April 2026?

Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Ahmedabad get the 50% limit. All other cities get 40%.

How is HRA exemption calculated?

It is the lowest of actual HRA received, rent paid minus 10% of salary, and 50% of salary in the eight cities (40% elsewhere). Salary means basic plus eligible DA.

Can I claim HRA in the new tax regime?

No. HRA exemption is available only if you choose the old regime.

Is landlord PAN mandatory for HRA?

Your employer needs the landlord's PAN if the rent you pay is more than ₹1 lakh a year.

Can I pay rent to my parents and claim HRA?

Yes, if they own the property, you actually pay the rent, preferably by bank transfer, and they show the rent as income in their tax return.

What is the maximum deduction under 80GG?

₹5,000 a month or ₹60,000 a year, subject to the 25% of income and rent minus 10% of income limits.
