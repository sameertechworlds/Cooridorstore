Accounting Games

Take a short break, refresh your mind and sharpen your accounting basics at the same time.

Learn accounting the fun way

These free accounting games turn the basics into quick challenges. They are useful for commerce students, accounts staff, and anyone who wants a short, useful break from work.

The games

  • Debit or Credit: a 60-second speed game on the golden rules of accounting. Assets and expenses increase on the debit side; liabilities, capital and income increase on the credit side.
  • Journal Entry: pick the correct journal entry for everyday transactions such as rent, credit sales, purchases with GST, contra entries and depreciation.
  • GST Speed Math: add GST, find GST and extract GST from inclusive amounts against the clock. Remember: GST inside an inclusive amount = Total × Rate ÷ (100 + Rate).
  • Memory Match: a relaxing card game that pairs tax short forms like ITC, TDS and IRN with their full forms.

Quick rules to remember
