What is a SIP?

  
A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) lets you invest a fixed amount in a mutual fund every month. Because each month's money keeps earning returns on top of earlier returns, small regular investments can grow into a large amount over time. This is the power of compounding.

  
SIP formula

  
FV = P × [((1 + i)n − 1) ÷ i] × (1 + i)

  
P is the monthly investment, i is the monthly return (annual return ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of months. With a step-up, the monthly amount rises every year, so we calculate it month by month.

  
Example

  
₹5,000 a month for 10 years at 12%: you invest ₹6,00,000 and the estimated value is about ₹11.62 lakh, so estimated returns are about ₹5.62 lakh.

  
Why step-up your SIP?

  
Raising your SIP by even 10% every year, in line with salary hikes, can make the final amount much larger than a flat SIP. Try it with the step-up slider above.
