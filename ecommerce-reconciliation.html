3. Payouts vs bankEach payout matched to a bank credit by UTR, amount and date.
4. Fees vs booksCommission, shipping, other fees and GST on fees against your ledgers.

How net payout is calculated

Net payout = Sale value − commission − shipping − other fees − GST on fees − TCS − TDS − refunds

The waterfall in the result shows each of these steps for all your platforms together, and the amount actually traced in the bank.

Frequently asked questions

1. What is e-commerce reconciliation?

It is matching what a marketplace reports with what is in your books: orders against your sales register, payouts against your bank statement, and fees and TCS against your ledgers. Any difference shows sales you missed, money not received or fees booked wrongly.

2. Which platforms does this tool support?

Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra and JioMart have their own pages, and any other marketplace or your own website works too. Just write the platform name in the Platform column of the template.

3. Can I reconcile all marketplaces together?

Yes. Put all platforms in one template with a Platform column. The summary shows platform sales, books sales, fees, TCS, payouts and bank receipts for each platform side by side.

4. Do I have to use the template?

No. You can upload the reports exactly as downloaded from the marketplace. The tool finds the header row and columns automatically, and you can change any column it picked.

5. Why does platform sales not match my books?

Common reasons are invoices not recorded, returns without credit notes, different invoice or order numbers, or wrong amounts. The Orders vs books tab lists each of these.

6. How is the payout to the bank checked?

Settlement lines are grouped by UTR, or by payout date when there is no UTR. Each group total is matched with a bank credit of the same amount within a few days, preferring credits whose narration has the platform name or UTR.

7. What is the GST TCS rate on e-commerce sales?

0.5% of the net taxable value under Section 52 of the CGST Act, since 10 July 2024. The TCS tab compares expected TCS, TCS in the settlement and GSTR-8 report, and TCS in your books.

8. What TDS do marketplaces deduct?

Income tax TDS at 0.1% of gross sales (earlier Section 194-O). The TCS & TDS tab shows it against your books.

9. Can I download the result?

Yes. Download Excel gives a summary sheet plus separate sheets for orders vs books, payments vs orders, payouts vs bank, fees and TCS.

10. Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Files are read inside your browser and never leave your computer.
