About Us

Simple, free and private tools for everyday tax and accounting work in India.

Why we built this

Every month, business owners, accountants and salaried people in India spend hours on the same tasks: working out GST, comparing tax regimes, reconciling GSTR-2B, matching bank statements and entering invoices in Tally. Most tools for this are either paid, need a login, or are hard to use.

cooridorstore.in brings these tools together in one place. They are free, need no signup, and are built to be quick and easy to understand.

Who is behind it

The website is built and maintained by an accounts and tax professional who handles bookkeeping, GST returns and reconciliations for businesses every day. The tools are designed around real problems faced in that work, and every calculation shows its working so you can check it yourself.

Your data stays with you

All calculators and file tools run inside your own browser. The Excel, Word and other files you choose are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our server.

What you can do here

  • Tax calculators: GST, GST late fee and interest, income tax (old vs new regime), HRA and TDS
  • Loan and investment: EMI calculator with repayment schedule and SIP calculator with step-up
  • Reconciliation: GSTR-2B, GSTR-1 vs e-invoice and bank reconciliation
  • Generators: GST invoice, rent receipts for HRA and Excel to Tally import
  • PDF tools: bank statement PDF to Excel, Word to PDF, PDF to Word, compress PDF and merge PDF
  • Learn and unwind: GST & tax quiz and accounting games
