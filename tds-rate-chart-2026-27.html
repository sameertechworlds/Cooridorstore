TDS Rate Chart FY 2026-27 under Section 393 of the Income-tax Act, 2025

TDS rate chart FY 2026-27 under new section 393: rent, professional fees, contractor, commission, interest and property, with old section, limits and due dates.

Updated 28 November 2026 · 8 min read · By the Cooridor Store team

Key takeaways

  • From 1 April 2026, most non-salary TDS is in one section, 393, of the Income-tax Act, 2025. Salary TDS is in section 392.
  • Rates and limits are largely the same as FY 2025-26. Only the section numbers changed.
  • Deposit TDS by the 7th of the next month (30 April for March).
  • Without the payee's PAN, TDS is generally 20% or the normal rate, if higher.

What changed in 2026-27

Under the Income-tax Act, 1961, TDS was spread over more than 40 sections from 192 to 194T. The Income-tax Act, 2025, in force from 1 April 2026, moved almost all non-salary TDS into section 393, organised as tables, and salary TDS into section 392. Payments made or credited on or after 1 April 2026 follow the new Act. Corrections for FY 2025-26 still use the old section numbers.

Interest and late fees

  • Not deducted: 1% per month from the date it should have been deducted.
  • Deducted but not deposited: 1.5% per month from the date of deduction.
  • Late TDS statement: ₹200 per day until filed, capped at the TDS amount.
  • No PAN: TDS at 20%, or the normal rate if higher (5% for e-commerce and goods purchase cases).

Frequently asked questions

What is section 393 of the Income-tax Act, 2025?

It is the single section that covers TDS on non-salary payments from 1 April 2026, replacing sections 193 to 194T of the 1961 Act.

What is the TDS rate on rent in FY 2026-27?

10% on land, building or furniture and 2% on plant and machinery, if rent is more than ₹50,000 a month. Individuals not under audit deduct 2% above ₹50,000 a month.

What is the TDS rate on professional fees?

10% if payments to a professional exceed ₹50,000 in a year. Technical services attract 2%.

What is the TDS limit on bank interest for senior citizens?

₹1 lakh a year. For others it is ₹50,000 for bank and post office interest.

When is TDS due for deposit?

By the 7th of the following month. TDS deducted in March can be deposited by 30 April.

What is the TDS rate if PAN is not available?

Generally 20% or the normal rate, whichever is higher.
