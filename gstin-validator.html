GSTIN format explained

Every GST Identification Number (GSTIN) has 15 characters. Taking 27AAPFU0939F1ZV as an example:

CharactersExampleMeaning
1–227State code (27 = Maharashtra)
3–12AAPFU0939FPAN of the business
131Entity number for this PAN in the state
14ZDefault letter for regular taxpayers
15VCheck character, worked out from the first 14

What this tool checks

  • Length and characters: exactly 15 letters and digits, with common mistakes such as spaces removed.
  • State code: one of the valid GST state and union territory codes.
  • PAN structure: five letters, four digits and one letter, plus the entity type from the 4th character.
  • Check character: recalculated from the first 14 characters. If it does not match, the tool shows what it should be, which usually points to a typing mistake.

A correctly formed GSTIN is not proof that the registration is active. Before claiming input tax credit or making a large payment, confirm the legal name and status on the GST portal using Search Taxpayer.

Frequently asked questions

1. What is the format of a GSTIN?

A GSTIN has 15 characters: a 2-digit state code, the 10-character PAN of the business, 1 entity number for registrations under the same PAN in a state, the letter Z by default, and 1 check character.

2. How do I check if a GST number is valid?

Type or paste the GSTIN in the box above. The tool checks the length, the state code, the PAN structure and the last check character, which is calculated from the first 14 characters.

3. Does this tool confirm that the GSTIN is active?

No. It checks whether the number is correctly formed. To see the legal name, status and return filing, use Search Taxpayer on the GST portal.

4. What is the GSTIN check digit?

The 15th character is a checksum worked out from the first 14 characters. If any character is typed wrongly, the checksum usually stops matching, so most typing mistakes are caught.

5. How do I find the PAN from a GSTIN?

Characters 3 to 12 of a regular GSTIN are the PAN of the business. The tool shows it, along with the type of entity from the 4th character of the PAN.

6. What does the 13th character of a GSTIN mean?

It is the entity number. It is 1 for the first registration under a PAN in a state, 2 for the second, and so on.

7. Why is Z the 14th character in a GSTIN?

Z is the default letter for regular taxpayers. Some special registrations, such as those of tax deductors, use a different letter.

8. Can I check many GSTINs at once?

Yes. Paste a list or upload an Excel or CSV file. The tool finds every GSTIN in the file, checks each one, marks duplicates and lets you download the result in Excel.

9. Why should I validate GSTINs of suppliers and customers?

A wrong GSTIN on an invoice means your customer cannot claim input tax credit, and a wrong supplier GSTIN in your books breaks GSTR-2B matching. Checking before filing GSTR-1 avoids amendments and disputes.

10. Is my data safe?

Yes. All checks run inside your browser. GSTINs and files are never sent to any server.
