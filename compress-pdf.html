How to reduce PDF file size

  1. Choose your PDF or drag it into the box.
  2. Pick a level. Recommended works best for scanned documents and photos.
  3. Press Compress PDF and download the smaller file.

Why compress PDFs?

Many government and bank portals limit the size of uploads, and email attachments are often capped too. Large scanned PDFs can be several MB. Compressing them helps you upload replies to GST notices, income tax documents, loan papers and KYC without errors.

FAQ

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF is read and compressed in your browser, so it is safe for financial and personal documents.

Why did my file not get smaller?

Text-only PDFs are usually already small. Try Basic, or keep the original file.
