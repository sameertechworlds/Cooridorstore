What is TDS?

    
Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) means the payer cuts a part of the payment as tax and deposits it with the government. The receiver can see it in Form 26AS and claim credit while filing the return.

    
Formula

    
TDS = Payment amount × TDS rate ÷ 100
Net payable = Payment amount − TDS

If the payee does not give PAN, TDS is usually deducted at 20%. From 1 April 2026, the Income-tax Act, 2025 replaced the 1961 Act and section numbers have been renumbered; the familiar old numbers are shown here for easy reference.

    
Worked example

    
A company pays ₹1,00,000 as professional fees to a CA. TDS under 194J = 1,00,000 × 10% = ₹10,000. The CA receives ₹90,000.
