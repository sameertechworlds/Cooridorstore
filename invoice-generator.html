What must a GST invoice contain?

  • Supplier name, address and GSTIN
  • Unique invoice number and date
  • Buyer name, address and GSTIN (if registered)
  • HSN or SAC code, quantity and value of goods or services
  • Rate and amount of CGST, SGST or IGST
  • Total value in figures and words, and signature

How to use this invoice generator

  1. Enter your business details once; they are saved on your device.
  2. Add the party details, invoice number and date.
  3. Add items with HSN, quantity, rate and GST rate.
  4. Click Download PDF and choose Save as PDF.
