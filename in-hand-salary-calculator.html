CTC vs gross salary vs in-hand salary

  
    
   
  • CTC (cost to company): everything the employer spends on you, including employer PF and gratuity.
    • 
   
  • Gross salary: CTC minus employer PF and gratuity. This is what appears as earnings on your payslip.
    • 
   
  • In-hand (take-home) salary: gross salary minus your PF, professional tax and income tax (TDS).
    • 
  

  
In-hand = CTC − employer PF − gratuity − employee PF − professional tax − income tax

  
Example: ₹12 lakh CTC

  
With 50% basic, PF capped at ₹1,800 a month and gratuity in CTC: employer PF is ₹21,600 and gratuity ₹28,860, so gross salary is ₹11,49,540. In the new regime, taxable income after the ₹75,000 standard deduction is ₹10,74,540, which is under ₹12 lakh, so income tax is nil. After your PF of ₹21,600 and professional tax of ₹2,400, the in-hand salary is about ₹93,795 a month.

Frequently asked questions

  
How can I increase my in-hand salary?

  
Ask for PF on the capped basic of ₹15,000 instead of full basic, and compare regimes. In the old regime, claim HRA, 80C and 80D properly.

  
Why is professional tax deducted?

  
It is a state tax on employment, up to ₹2,500 a year. Some states, like Delhi, do not charge it.
