Merge PDF Files

Combine several PDFs into one file. Change the order, choose pages, and download. Your files never leave your device.

How to merge PDF files

  1. Click the box above or drag your PDF files into it.
  2. Use ↑ and ↓ to set the order. The first file comes first in the merged PDF.
  3. To take only some pages from a file, type them in its pages box, for example 1-3,5. Leave it blank for all pages.
  4. Press Merge PDFs. The combined file downloads as merged.pdf.

Why merge PDFs here?

  • Private: files are joined inside your browser and are never uploaded. Safe for invoices, bank statements, ITR and client documents.
  • Free and unlimited: no signup, no watermark, no daily limit.
  • Page control: pick exactly the pages you need from each file.

Common uses

Combine monthly purchase invoices into one file for your CA, join bank statements for a loan application, attach supporting documents to a GST reply, or put scanned KYC pages together.

FAQ

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

Remove the password first by opening the PDF with its password and saving or printing it as a new PDF, then merge.

Is there a size limit?

There is no fixed limit. Very large files depend on your device's memory.
