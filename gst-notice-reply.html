What is a GST notice reply generator?

How do I reply to a GST notice on the portal?

  1. Log in to the GST portal and open the notice (Services › User Services › View Notices and Orders, or View Additional Notices/Orders; for DRC-01B and DRC-01C, Services › Returns › Return Compliance).
  2. If you accept any amount, pay it first through DRC-03 and note the ARN.
  3. Download the reply from this tool as PDF and attach it with your reconciliations and documents.
  4. Paste the short summary in the reply text box, submit with DSC or EVC, and save the acknowledgement.

What happens if I do not reply to a GST notice?

The officer can decide the matter on the basis of the records available. An unanswered ASMT-10 can lead to a show cause notice, an unanswered DRC-01 can lead to a demand order with interest and penalty, and an unanswered REG-17 or REG-31 can lead to cancellation of registration. For DRC-01B and DRC-01C, GSTR-1 filing for the next period is blocked until you reply or pay.

Frequently asked questions

1. How do I reply to a GST notice?

Find the form, period, points raised and deadline in the notice. Prepare a point-wise reply with reconciliations, pay any accepted amount through DRC-03, and file the reply in the matching form (ASMT-11, DRC-06, Part B of DRC-01B/01C, REG-18 and so on) before the due date.

2. What is the time limit to reply to ASMT-10?

30 days from the date of service of the notice, or such further period as the officer allows, under Rule 99 of the CGST Rules. The reply is filed in ASMT-11.

3. What happens if I do not reply to DRC-01B or DRC-01C?

Both must be answered in Part B within 7 days. If you neither reply nor pay, filing of GSTR-1 or IFF for the next period is blocked under Rule 59(6).

4. What is the difference between DRC-01A and DRC-01?

DRC-01A is an intimation before a show cause notice that lets you pay or explain early. DRC-01 is the summary of the show cause notice itself, and its reply is filed in DRC-06.

5. Is penalty payable if I pay the tax after a show cause notice?

Under Section 73, no penalty if tax and interest are paid within 30 days of the notice. For FY 2024-25 onwards, Section 74A gives 60 days in non-fraud cases. In fraud cases, a reduced penalty of 25% applies if paid within the allowed period. Use the calculator above to see the figures.
