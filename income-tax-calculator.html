Salaried people get a standard deduction of ₹75,000. If taxable income is up to ₹12 lakh, a rebate makes the tax zero, so a salaried person earning up to ₹12.75 lakh pays no tax.

The old regime allows deductions such as 80C (up to ₹1.5 lakh), 80D, HRA and home loan interest, plus a ₹50,000 standard deduction. Income up to ₹5 lakh gets a full rebate. A 4% health and education cess is added in both regimes.


    
Worked example

    
Salary ₹15,00,000, new regime: taxable income = 15,00,000 − 75,000 = ₹14,25,000. Tax = 0 + 20,000 + 40,000 + 33,750 = ₹93,750. Cess 4% = ₹3,750. Total = ₹97,500.


    
Which regime should I choose?

    
If your total deductions are small, the new regime usually saves more. If you claim large deductions like HRA, 80C and home loan interest, compare both using the calculator above.
