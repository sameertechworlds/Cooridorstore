Worked example

    
Salary ₹15,00,000, new regime: taxable income = 15,00,000 − 75,000 = ₹14,25,000. Tax = 0 + 20,000 + 40,000 + 33,750 = ₹93,750. Cess 4% = ₹3,750. Total = ₹97,500.


    
Which regime should I choose?

    
If your total deductions are small, the new regime usually saves more. If you claim large deductions like HRA, 80C and home loan interest, compare both using the calculator above.

    
Frequently asked questions

    
1. How do I calculate income tax for FY 2026-27?

    
Subtract the standard deduction (and, in the old regime, your other deductions) from your income to get taxable income. Apply the slab rates, subtract any rebate, then add 4% cess. The calculator above shows every step.

    
2. Is income up to ₹12 lakh tax-free?

    
Yes, in the new regime. Taxable income up to ₹12 lakh gets a full rebate. For salaried people, the ₹75,000 standard deduction makes income up to ₹12.75 lakh tax-free.

    
3. What is marginal relief in the new regime?

    
If taxable income is slightly above ₹12 lakh, the tax cannot be more than the income above ₹12 lakh. For example, on ₹12,10,000 taxable income the tax is limited to ₹10,000 plus cess.

    
4. How much tax is payable on a ₹10 lakh salary?

    
Nil in the new regime, as taxable income after the ₹75,000 standard deduction is ₹9,25,000, which is covered by the rebate.

    
5. How much tax is payable on a ₹15 lakh salary?

    
In the new regime, taxable income is ₹14,25,000. Tax is ₹93,750 plus ₹3,750 cess, a total of ₹97,500.

    
6. How much tax is payable on a ₹20 lakh salary?

    
In the new regime, taxable income is ₹19,25,000. Tax is ₹1,85,000 plus ₹7,400 cess, a total of ₹1,92,400.

    
7. Which deductions are allowed in the new regime?

    
Only a few, mainly the ₹75,000 standard deduction for salaried people and the employer's contribution to NPS. Deductions like 80C, 80D, HRA and home loan interest on a self-occupied house are allowed only in the old regime.

    
8. What is the standard deduction?

    
A flat deduction from salary or pension income, without any proof. It is ₹75,000 in the new regime and ₹50,000 in the old regime.

    
9. Can I switch between the old and new regime?

    
Salaried people without business income can choose either regime every year while filing the return. People with business or professional income can switch back to the new regime only once.

    
10. What are surcharge and cess?

    
A 4% health and education cess is added to the tax in both regimes. Surcharge applies only above ₹50 lakh income: 10% above ₹50 lakh, 15% above ₹1 crore and 25% above ₹2 crore in the new regime.
